---
article: "216"
title: "Constitution of High Courts"
part: "VI"
part_title: "THE STATES IN PART A OF THE FIRST SCHEDULE"
---
Every High Court shall consist of a Chief Justice and such other Judges as the President may from time to time deem it necessary to appoint.

Provided that the Judges so appointed shall at no time exceed in number such maximum number as the President may, from time to time, by order fix in relation to that Court.
