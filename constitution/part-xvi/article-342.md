---
article: "342"
title: "Scheduled Tribes"
part: "XVI"
part_title: "SPECIAL PROVISIONS RELATING TO CERTAIN CLASSES"
---
(1) The President may, after consultation with the Governor or Rajpramukh of State, by public notification6,5 specify the tribes or tribal communities or parts of or groups within tribes or tribal communities which shall for the purposes of this Constitution be deemed to be Scheduled Tribes in relation to that State

(2) Parliament may by law include in or exclude from the list of Scheduled Tribes specified in a notification issued under clause (1) any tribe or tribal community or part of or group within any tribe or tribal community, but save as aforesaid a notification issued under the said clause shall not be varied by any subsequent notification.
